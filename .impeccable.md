## Design Context

### Users
Pluto Booking serves customers comparing and booking cars, apartments, hotel rooms, and private stays, plus partners managing listings and reservations. Interfaces must support quick scanning, confident decisions, and clear recovery from errors across mobile and desktop.

### Brand Personality
Premium, trustworthy, and direct. The experience should feel polished and distinctive without becoming decorative, loud, or difficult to operate.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a light, refined visual system built around Outfit and Pluto Booking's primary color `#02006c`. Favor precise alignment, tinted white surfaces, restrained glass effects, purposeful shadows, and clean iconography. Avoid gradient backgrounds, oversized typography, excessive cards, one-color layouts, and generic marketing composition.

### Design Principles
- Make status, hierarchy, and the next action immediately understandable.
- Use brand color and motion purposefully for feedback, focus, and active state.
- Keep typography compact, readable, and consistent with Outfit.
- Preserve accessibility with strong contrast, visible focus, reduced-motion support, and touch-friendly controls.
- Keep shared components isolated, reusable, responsive, and inexpensive to render.
